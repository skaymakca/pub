Every sight on the plan, with the official ticketing link and a map pin. The ticketing URLs were checked, not remembered — these are exactly the ones reseller sites imitate.

Florence Thu 15 afternoon → Fri 16 morning

WhatWhy it is on the listBookMap
Galleria dell'AccademiaMichelangelo's David, plus the unfinished Prisoners straining out of their blocks in the corridor leading to him. About an hour. Timed entry.b-ticket.commap
Brunelleschi's DomeThe cathedral dome, raised without scaffolding and still the largest masonry dome ever built. 463 steps between the inner and outer shells. Timed, name-matched, non-refundable.tickets.duomo.firenze.itmap
Baptistery of San GiovanniOlder than the cathedral it faces. Ghiberti's bronze doors outside, a blazing gold mosaic ceiling inside. Twenty minutes, included in the Duomo pass.duomo.firenze.itmap
Ponte VecchioThe medieval bridge of goldsmiths' shops, the only one in Florence the retreating Germans left standing in 1944. Free, best at dusk.—map
Uffizi — not in the planBotticelli, Leonardo, Caravaggio. Left out deliberately: 2–3 hours, and it would swallow either the Thursday afternoon or the Friday morning whole. Here in case you want to trade.b-ticket.commap

Rome — ancient Tue Oct 20

WhatWhy it is on the listBookMap
ColosseumThe 50,000-seat amphitheatre of AD 80. One ticket also covers the Forum and Palatine within 24 hours of your slot. €18 + €2 booking.ticketing.colosseo.itmap
Roman ForumThe civic centre of the republic and empire — senate house, temples, the road every triumph came down. Walk in from the Colosseum on the same ticket.ticketing.colosseo.itmap
Palatine HillThe hill above the Forum where the emperors actually lived, and where the founding myth puts Romulus. Same ticket. First thing to drop if legs are going.ticketing.colosseo.itmap
Capitoline MuseumsThe world's oldest public museum. The bronze she-wolf, the original equestrian Marcus Aurelius, and a terrace looking back over the Forum you have just walked.museicapitolini.orgmap
PantheonNearly two thousand years old and still the largest unreinforced concrete dome on earth, with the oculus open to the sky. Free for under-18s — but every visitor needs a booked slot.portale.museiitaliani.itmap
Trevi FountainBaroque, enormous, jammed at every hour — but genuinely better late at night. Free.—map
Piazza NavonaBuilt on the footprint of a Roman stadium, which is why it is that shape. Bernini's Four Rivers fountain in the middle. Free.—map

The Vatican Wed Oct 21

WhatWhy it is on the listBookMap
Papal General AudienceWednesday mornings in St Peter's Square when the Pope is in Rome. Free, but tickets must be requested in advance and collected the afternoon before.eventi.pontificalisdomus.vamap
St Peter's BasilicaThe largest church in the world. Michelangelo's Pietà is immediately inside on the right, behind glass. Entry is free — book only to skip the security queue.basilicasanpietro.vamap
Vatican Museums & Sistine ChapelMiles of galleries ending at Michelangelo's ceiling. Open to 20:00, last entry 18:00, and afternoons are markedly quieter than the morning crush. Timed slot essential.museivaticani.vamap
Dome of St Peter's — closed Weds551 steps, or a lift to the roof then 320. Not available on your Vatican day. Listed so the trade-off is on the record.basilicasanpietro.vamap

On the way Orvieto · Siena · Panzano

WhatWhy it is on the listBookMap
Duomo di OrvietoA striped Gothic cathedral with a mosaic front like a jewellery cabinet, and Signorelli's apocalypse frescoes inside. €8 covers the cathedral, the Emilio Greco museum and the underground chambers.duomodiorvieto.itmap
Orvieto Underground1,200 caves, wells, tunnels and pigeon lofts cut into the tufa beneath the town over roughly three millennia. One hour, guided. Tickets at Piazza del Duomo 23.—map
Duomo di SienaBlack-and-white striped piers, a Pisano pulpit, and a floor of inlaid marble panels uncovered only part of the year. The better cathedral of the two you will see.operaduomo.siena.itmap
Piazza del CampoThe shell-shaped square the Palio is run around, sloping down to the town hall. Free, and the best place in Siena to sit still.—map
Officina della BisteccaDario Cecchini's grill room above the butcher shop in Panzano. One seating, one menu, wine included, €50 a head. Aperitivo 19:00, table 19:30. Runs every day.dariocecchini.commap
Tenuta di MonacianoThe wedding. An 18th-century villa and five restored farmhouses on a working Chianti Classico estate at Ponte a Bozzone, 7 km from Siena.—map
