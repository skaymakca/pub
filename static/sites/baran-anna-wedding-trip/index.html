The trip in one paragraph

Fly into Zadar on 8 October and spend four days with Marina's family. On the 13th, drive down to Split and take a one-hour hop to Rome, then work north through Orvieto and the Val d'Orcia to Florence. Friday the 16th, down to Tenuta di Monaciano near Siena for the welcome party, the wedding on Saturday, brunch and Dario Cecchini on Sunday. Monday morning in Siena, then the bus to Rome for three days — ancient Rome on the Tuesday, the Vatican on the Wednesday — and home on the nonstop on the 22nd.

Five of us for the wedding: Sukru, Marina, Luka, Maja and Zehra. Four for Croatia — Zehra joins in Florence on the 15th.

Three things worth knowing up front

Croatia is nearly free to add. Flying an open jaw into Zadar and home from Rome costs $69 a head more than simply flying to Rome and back. With the Split–Rome hop it is about $147 a person — and the family put you up. The constraint on Croatia is days, not dollars.

The venue is at the Siena end of Chianti, not the Florence end. Monaciano is 7 km from Siena and 1h30 from Florence. That is why Florence sits on Thursday and Friday morning, and why Monday is a Siena morning and a direct bus south.

Two of the three wedding days are already spoken for. Pizza and spritzes at 17:30 on the Friday, the wedding on Saturday, brunch on Sunday morning. The only unclaimed slot in the whole weekend is Sunday evening, which is where Cecchini goes.
