Zehra, separately: Delta nonstop BOS→FCO on 15 October, 16:45 → 06:50+1, $539 — she lands Friday morning and you collect her at Fiumicino on the way north. She flies home from Bologna with everyone. The cheaper $451 fare lands at 15:35 on the 16th, which does not make the 17:30 welcome. Her open jaw has not been priced as a single ticket yet and will probably beat two one-ways.

Why Split and not Zadar. The Zadar routing that shaped the original plan only exists on 8 October. Move a day either side and it becomes two stops and thirteen hours. Split holds a single-stop at 11h10 and is cheaper than the Zadar flight was.

Why Bologna and not Florence. $3 cheaper, 41 minutes faster, and on a runway that works — Florence has 1,688 m hemmed in by terrain and the motorway, and diverts to Pisa or Bologna in strong wind. Losing a transatlantic connection to a gust is not worth the fifteen minutes Florence saves on the ground.

Fifteen days

Day 1Oct 7Wed
BOS → AMS

Evening departure, 18:55. Overnight to Amsterdam, one stop, 11h10. School is a half day, not an absence.

Day 2Oct 8Thu
Split → Zadar

On the ground at 12:05. Collected by Marina's family and driven up the coast to Zadar, about 1h35 on the A1.

Flying into Split rather than Zadar is what makes the earlier departure work at all — see why this shape.

Day 3–5Oct 9–11Fri–Sun
Zadar — family

Three full days with Marina's side. Not planned here and not needing to be. Olive and grape harvest is on all month.

Day 6Oct 12Mon
Zadar → Split → Rome

Back down to Split for the 16:10, into Fiumicino at 17:10. Into the city by about 18:30 — no car, none of the Rome days need one.

Evening with nothing booked. The Trevi Fountain and the Pantheon are both better after dark and both cost nothing to stand in front of.

The only hard number on the Croatia side: be at Split airport by 14:05.

Day 7Oct 13Tue
The Vatican

St Peter's Basilica — free entry, and Michelangelo's Pietà is just inside on the right, behind glass. Then the dome: 551 steps, or a lift to the roof and then 320. The upper stretch leans with the curve of the dome, which is the bit they will remember.

Afternoon: the Vatican Museums and the Sistine Chapel. Open to 20:00 with last entry at 18:00, and the afternoon is markedly quieter than the morning scrum.

Whether to climb the dome is open — as is Brunelleschi’s in Florence on the 19th. Neither is booked, and they are a real choice rather than two ticks on a list: see the comparison.

If a Papal Audience is confirmed for the 14th, this day and the Naples day swap — see the Naples day.

Vatican Museums slot — book the afternoon

Day 8Oct 14Wed
Pompeii & Vesuvius

The one day that had to land here. Pompeii's summer hours run to 14 October inclusive — closing 19:00 rather than 17:00. One day later and the site shuts two hours earlier with a 15:30 last entry.

Termini 06:50 → Napoli Centrale 08:05. Vesuvius 09:45–11:45 — the shuttle to the car park at 1,000 m, then a 30-minute gravel climb to the crater rim. Then down to Pompeii by 13:00, and you have it until seven.

If you take a group tour this stops having to be a Wednesday — the long afternoon is only worth anything if you are staying for it. Book a driver to meet the train at Napoli Centrale rather than doing this on the Circumvesuviana — with two timed tickets and two children you do not want that line in the critical path. There are nine ways to build this day and they differ by a factor of four in price: see the Naples day.

Vesuvius is online booking only and shuts for wind — there is no second slot on this shape

No under-18 exemption at Pompeii for non-EU visitors. About €100 for five to enter.

Day 9Oct 15Thu
Ancient Rome

Colosseum on a timed morning slot, then the Roman Forum and Palatine Hill — one ticket covers all three within 24 hours of your Colosseum time.

That is a great deal of walking on rough ground. Break it after the Forum. The Capitoline Museums sit right above it — the she-wolf, the bronze Marcus Aurelius, the best view back down — then the Pantheon.

A deliberately soft last day in Rome before Friday's drive north and the wedding weekend.

Colosseum tickets release exactly 30 days ahead — Mon 15 September

Park closes 18:30 in October, last admission 17:30

Day 10Oct 16Fri

Zehra lands FCO 06:50. Collect her, pick up the car at the airport, and go — Monaciano is 2h40 north.

At the estate by about midday. Lunch there, unpack, walk the vineyard, change. 17:30 — pizza and spritzes. Four hours of slack in the day.

Car picked up here, not on arrival — six rental days become four

Day 11Oct 17Sat
THE WEDDING

The reason for all of it. Nothing else goes on this day.

Day 12Oct 18Sun
Wedding brunch Chianti → Panzano

The brunch takes the morning. That is the day's anchor.

The afternoon is free and it points the right way — Cecchini is at 19:30 in Panzano, forty minutes north, so you are travelling in that direction anyway. Castello di Brolio is 18 minutes from the estate and 40 on to Panzano; Badia a Coltibuono is 19 and 31.

Officina della Bistecca at 19:30, aperitivo at 19:00. One seating, one menu, wine included, €50 a head, kids' menu.

Book a driver — the SR222 back at eleven is unlit, narrow and full of switchbacks. An NCC runs roughly €130–160 for the round trip.

Day 13Oct 19Mon
Siena → Florence

Check out about 09:30 and drive twenty minutes into Siena. Park outside the walls, bags locked in the car, and walk in.

★ The cathedral floor is uncovered. All 56 inlaid marble panels, 18 August to 15 November for the St Francis anniversary — in a normal year it would have been covered again the day before you arrived.

Then the Campo, a long lunch, and north to Florence, about 1h30, arriving mid-afternoon. The whole Duomo complex is open on Mondays — the dome until 18:45, the Baptistery until 19:30 — so the afternoon is not wasted.

Drop the car in Florence. Only the Accademia and the Uffizi shut on Mondays.

Day 14Oct 20Tue
LUKA'S BIRTHDAY Florence — or Maranello

Accademia at 08:15, first slot, for David.

After that the day is still open — the one decision left on this trip. Either a full Florence day with the Uffizi and the Opera del Duomo museum, or the Ferrari museum at Maranello, which sits directly on the line from Florence to your flight. Both end with a night in Florence or Bologna respectively.

See after the wedding for what each is.

Day 15Oct 21Wed

Bologna to Boston, 13:55 → 18:30, one stop, 10h35. Leave central Bologna about 11:15 — the Frecciarossa is 37 minutes and the Marconi Express another seven.
