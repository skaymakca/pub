Newton Public Schools 2026-27

What this costs in school

The district calendar has one day off in October, and it happens to land exactly where it does the most good.

The number that shapes this

October 2026 has exactly one day off: Monday 12 October, Indigenous People’s Day. Nothing else in the month — 21 school days, and the next holidays are not until Election Day on 3 November.

The outbound is an 18:55 flight, so departure day is an early pickup, not an absence. The return lands 18:30, so that day is gone entirely.

Three things that fall out of it

1. Shifting the whole trip a day earlier is free on this axis. Nine days either way. Moving out of Thursday-to-Thursday into Wednesday-to-Wednesday trades one weekday for another, so everything the earlier departure buys — the third Rome day, and Pompeii landing on its last summer-hours date — costs nothing in school.

2. Croatia still costs only two school days. Italy alone would be seven. The Saturday, Sunday and the holiday on the 12th form a three-day free block sitting squarely in the Zadar leg. Same answer the money gave.

3. The one day that would have helped was Luka’s birthday. Coming home on Tuesday the 20th saves a school day and about $43. It also puts an eleven-year-old on a transatlantic flight on his birthday, which settled it.

Worth doing

Nine or ten days is about five per cent of the 180-day year. Notify both principals in advance — Mason-Rice for Maja, Countryside for Luka. Newton generally wants extended absences flagged rather than phoned in each morning, and doing it early makes the work-to-take-along conversation easier.

Elementary early release is Wednesdays at 12:20, which is what makes the outbound Wednesday a genuinely cheap day to leave on.
