Newton Public Schools 2026-27

What this costs in school

The district calendar has one day off in October, and it happens to land exactly where it does the most good.

The number that shapes this

October 2026 has exactly one day off: Monday 12 October, Indigenous People’s Day. Nothing else in the month — 21 school days, and the next holidays are not until Election Day on 3 November. But that one day lands on the Monday of the Zadar leg, immediately after a weekend, which changes the arithmetic more than you would expect.

The outbound is a 17:45 flight, so departure day is an early pickup, not an absence — they can do most of it. The return lands 16:43, so that day is gone entirely.

Three things that fall out of it

1. Croatia costs two school days. Italy alone would be seven; the full trip is nine. Saturday the 10th, Sunday the 11th and the holiday on the 12th form a three-day free block that lands squarely in the middle of the Zadar leg. That is the same answer the money gave — Croatia adds $69 a head in airfare and two school days. It is cheap on both counts.

2. Trimming barely helps. The 13-day version saves one school day and the 12-day saves two, in exchange for two or three days of trip. That is a poor rate, and it largely settles the 15-versus-13 question: the shorter versions were about money and time, and on the school axis they buy almost nothing.

3. Option 2 gets no rescue here. Also nine days missed despite being fifteen long, because it runs Saturday to Saturday. It stays worse on money and routing with nothing to set against it.

An idea worth killing in public

Leaving Friday 9 October instead of Thursday saves a school day and only costs one Zadar night. It looks like the obvious optimisation.

It is not, and the reason is the flights. The clean routing to Zadar exists only on the 8th — $578, one stop in Frankfurt, 10h 15m, on the ground at 10:00. On the 9th the choices are two stops through Frankfurt and Vienna at 13h 50m for $612, or a single stop carrying an eighteen-hour Frankfurt layover at 26h 50m.

Three and a half extra hours and a second connection, with two children, to save one day of fourth-grade. Thursday the 8th stands.

One thing to actually do

Nine or ten days is about 5% of the 180-day year. Tell both principals in advance rather than calling it in each morning — Newton wants extended absences flagged ahead of time, and it is a much easier conversation in September than on the day.
