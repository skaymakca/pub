The one unforgiving date

Monday 15 September, around 09:00 Rome time. Colosseum tickets release exactly 30 days ahead of the 15 October visit and the good slots go within hours. Everything else here can slip a week; that cannot. Sources differ on 08:45 versus 09:00 — be sitting there at 08:40.

Two ways to lose money on this trip

Buy the Colosseum from a reseller. Ticketing moved to ticketing.colosseo.it in April 2024 and the old CoopCulture links still circulate. Resellers charge €35 and up for an €18 ticket, and some of what they sell is not a timed slot at all.

Miss the Vesuvius window. It is online-only, timed, capped, and the whole Wednesday is built around it. There is no second Wednesday on this trip — and if the weather shuts the mountain on the day, that is a refund question, not a rebooking one.

Two ways to lose money here

Buy from the official site, not the first search result. Paid search for "Colosseum tickets" is full of resellers holding separate allocations at €29+ against a €18 face value. The Colosseum moved off CoopCulture to ticketing.colosseo.it in April 2024, so older guides send you to the wrong place. The Duomo has publicly warned about the same thing — buy the Brunelleschi Pass at tickets.duomo.firenze.it and nowhere else.

The dome slot is not refundable and not changeable, and it is name-matched with ID checked at the door. Everything about Friday hangs off it, so book it only once the estate address and the drive time are confirmed.
